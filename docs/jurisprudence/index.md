# Jurisprudence (Law 301)

**100 marks, pass 35. Compulsory. 3 hours, any 5 of 7 questions.**

Jurisprudence is the theory of law. Other papers teach *what the law says* about contracts or courts. This paper asks bigger questions: what law is, where it comes from, why people obey it, and what basic ideas every legal system is built on, such as a right, a person, property and possession.

It feels abstract at first. It gets easier once you notice that the same few ideas come back in every other paper.
