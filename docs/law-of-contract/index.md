# Law of Contract (Law 305)

**100 marks, pass 35. Compulsory.** When a promise is legally binding, and the special contracts, under the Muluki Civil Code 2074.

3. Consideration and privity
4. Capacity to contract
5. Free consent: coercion, undue influence, misrepresentation, fraud, mistake
6. Legality of object and consideration
7. Indirect (quasi) contract
8. Contingent contract
9. Performance of contract
10. Termination of contract
11. Breach of contract and remedies

**Specific contracts**

12. Indemnity and guarantee
13. Bailment and pledge
14. Agency
15. Sale of goods
16. Carriage of goods
17. Arbitration
18. Hire purchase under the Muluki Civil Code 2074
19. Contract provisions of the Muluki Civil Code 2074
20. Prescribed cases (10)

## Asked most often

Essentials of a valid contract, agency, bailment (bailee's rights and duties, bailment vs pledge), privity of contract, free consent. Each year at least one question is on a prescribed case.
