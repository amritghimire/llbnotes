# Procedural Law (Law 302)

**100 marks, pass 35. Compulsory.** How a case moves through Nepal's courts, from filing to judgment, appeal, writ and execution.

!!! note "Notes for this paper are planned"
    The units below follow the syllabus. Full notes will be added unit by unit.

## Units

1. Introduction: meaning, nature and importance; procedural vs substantive law; general, summary and special procedure
2. General principles and recent trends: natural justice, locus standi, limitation, laches, stare decisis, res judicata, amicus curiae; Muluki Civil Procedure Code 2074; separate bench, continuous hearing, in-camera hearing, use of IT
3. Jurisdiction of courts, specialised courts and judicial bodies
4. Stages and techniques of original court proceedings (civil and criminal)
5. Alternative dispute resolution: arbitration and mediation
6. Appellate proceedings
7. Writ, review and revision proceedings
8. Execution of civil and criminal judgments
9. Prescribed cases (10)

## Asked most often

Jurisdiction (in 4 papers), natural justice (4), limitation and laches (3), stare decisis and precedent (3), amicus curiae (3), revision (3), execution of judgments (3), summary vs general procedure (3). Each year one question is on a prescribed case.
